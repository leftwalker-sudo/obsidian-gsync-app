Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

"Obsidian GSync (personal)" is an OAuth client used by a single person, its owner, to sync their own Obsidian notes with their own Google Drive.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission (Google Drive scope), the Obsidian GSync plugin reads and writes files in one folder of the owner's own Google Drive: My Drive/Obsidian/Leftwalker. No other Google data is used.

Where data goes

Files travel directly between the owner's devices and Google's servers. There is no third-party server, no analytics and no tracking. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own devices.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to anyone. No data from other Google users is accessed; the app is not offered to other users.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
